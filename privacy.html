Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Idemia Identity & Security LLC ("we", "us") collects and uses information in connection with our internal On-Call SMS Alert program (the "Service"), which delivers critical operational incident notifications to enrolled personnel.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the Service: the recipient's name and mobile telephone number, provided at the time of opt-in, and delivery metadata (such as message status and timestamps) returned by our messaging provider.

How We Use Information

Mobile numbers are used solely to send operational Sev1 (critical severity) incident alerts. We do not use this information for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers with third parties. Numbers are processed only by our messaging provider (Azure Communication Services) for the sole purpose of delivering the alerts you have opted in to receive. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Message Frequency & Charges

Message frequency varies based on incident volume. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. After opting out, you will no longer receive alert messages.

Data Retention

We retain mobile numbers only while a recipient remains enrolled in the Service. Numbers are removed promptly after a recipient opts out or leaves the on-call program.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to jpsdevops@ps-idemia.com.

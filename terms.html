These Terms & Conditions govern participation in the Idemia Identity & Security LLC On-Call SMS Alert program (the "Program").

Program Description

The Program sends critical operational Sev1 incident alerts via SMS to enrolled on-call personnel. Alerts are generated automatically by our monitoring systems and delivered through Azure Communication Services.

Opt-In / Consent

By enrolling through our opt-in form or by texting the keyword ONCALL to +1 (866) 319-7751, you consent to receive recurring SMS alerts at the mobile number you provide. Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on operational incident volume. There is no fixed number of messages per period.

Opt-Out & Help
